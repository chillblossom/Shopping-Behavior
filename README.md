# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?

How does the average Purchase Amount vary across different Age groups, 
and which demographic represents the highest overall transaction value?

In which Locations is Express Shipping Type most utilized, 
and does the choice of shipping method receive higher Review Ratings?

Do customers with a high number of Previous Purchases 
demonstrate a higher Frequency of Purchases compared to new customers, and does their Payment Method differ?

What is the top Item Purchased for each Gender, 
and is there a significant difference in the Purchase Amount between these groups?

Does the use of a Promo Code result in a higher Purchase Amount for specific Item Purchased 
compared to those bought at full price?

For the most popular Item Purchased, does the distribution of Sizes purchased,  shift significantly between Winter and Summer seasons?
